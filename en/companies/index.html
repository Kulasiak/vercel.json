Businesses

A service built for those who produce waste oil without making a business of it: you produce, we remove, you keep the proof.

Sectors

Who calls us

Garages & service stations

Drain oil, filters and contaminated packaging: regular collection and suitable containers.

Industry & manufacturing

Hydraulic, cutting and quenching oils, with volumes tracked per workshop.

Hotels & restaurants

Used frying oil: containers supplied, rotation matched to your activity.

Food processing

High, regular volumes with hygiene and material separation constraints.

Transport & fleets

Maintenance workshops, depots and life bases, including remote sites in the South.

Public bodies

Municipalities, hospitals, schools and universities: public tenders and agreements.

Process

How a contract works

  1. Site visit and diagnosis

    We measure real volumes and map collection points on site.

  2. Written proposal

    Frequency, containers, price and lead-time commitments, with no hidden clauses.

  3. Set-up

    Sealed bins and drums delivered and labelled, with a short briefing for your team.

  4. Scheduled collections

    Fixed dates or on call, with weighing and a transfer note every time.

  5. Compliance file

    Your documents are archived and handed over as an annual summary.

Your obligations, in plain terms

Whoever produces waste remains responsible for it until final disposal or recovery. In practice: know what you generate, store it properly, hand it to a licensed operator, and be able to prove it.

That is exactly what we document for you, collection after collection.

Producer checklist

  • Identify and classify your waste
  • Store on bunded areas, sheltered and unmixed
  • Never mix mineral and cooking oils
  • Contract with a licensed collector
  • Keep every transfer note
  • Maintain an up-to-date outgoing register
