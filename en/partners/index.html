Partners & institutions

No collection chain is built alone. We work with the authorities, technical bodies and professional organisations that shape the sector in Algeria.

Ecosystem

Who we work with

Environmental authorities

The environment ministry and wilaya environment directorates: permits and oversight.

National Waste Agency

Flow data, good practice and support in structuring collection chains.

CNTPP

Guiding manufacturers towards cleaner, less waste-intensive processes.

Trade associations

Restaurateurs, hoteliers, mechanics and hauliers: essential relays on the ground.

Municipalities

Drop-off points, neighbourhood campaigns and clean-up operations.

Research & startups

Labs and young companies working on biodiesel and oil regeneration.

What we bring to a partnership

A field operator, reliable collection data, and the ability to mobilise businesses across a defined territory.

We gladly take part in awareness drives, environment days and school programmes: that is where tomorrow's collection is won.
