Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often. If your answer is not here, give us a call.

Do I need to book?

For two or three people, usually not: there is normally a table free. On Friday and Saturday evenings, and for groups of six or more, it is better to call. A couple of minutes on the phone or a WhatsApp message is enough.

Do you have gluten-free dishes?

Yes, there is a dedicated section with pizza, pasta, a dessert and gluten-free beer, made with certified ingredients and separate equipment. The kitchen as a whole is not entirely gluten free, so please always tell the staff when you order.

Are there vegan and vegetarian options?

Yes, two whole sections of the menu. On top of that, many dishes in the other sections are already vegetarian: you can recognise them by the coloured labels on the menu.

How far are you from Termini station?

About five minutes on foot. Leaving Termini, take Via Giovanni Giolitti and turn into Via Principe Amedeo: we are at number 78/80.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, we accept debit cards, Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Satispay, with no minimum amount.

Do you do takeaway and delivery?

Yes, the whole menu is available to take away and we deliver in the surrounding streets. To order, just call or send a WhatsApp message.

Do you take groups?

Yes, we have a room that fits groups. For more than eight people, call us a day ahead so we can arrange the tables and, if you wish, an agreed menu.

Are dogs allowed?

Yes, dogs are welcome both inside and at the outdoor tables.

Is the menu available in other languages?

Yes, in nine languages: Italian, English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Polish and Greek. Scan the QR code on the table and the menu opens in your phone's language.

When is the kitchen open?

For lunch from 12:00 to 15:30 and for dinner from 18:30 to 23:00. Outside those hours the bar stays open for coffee, sandwiches and aperitivo.

Still looking for an answer?

Call us or write on WhatsApp: we reply straight away.
