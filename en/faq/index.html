Frequently asked questions

The questions we get most often, on the phone and on site.

Can I pour waste oil down the drain or burn it?
No. Unauthorised discharge and burning are prohibited and carry penalties, strengthened by Law 25-02. Oil must be handed to a licensed operator.
Is the service paid?
It depends on the stream. Cooking oil in regular volumes can be bought back; small volumes and associated waste involve a charged service. The quote is always in writing.
What is the minimum volume for a collection?
We run shared rounds: even a small producer can be added to an existing route.
What documents will I receive?
A transfer note at every visit stating type, quantity and destination, plus an annual summary.
Do you operate in the South?
Yes, in industrial and oil zones, at a frequency adapted to the distances. Contact us for your area's schedule.
Can engine oil and frying oil be mixed?
Never. Mixing makes the stream unrecoverable and can downgrade everything to hazardous waste. Always two separate containers.
What happens to the oil collected?
It goes to recovery: re-refining, energy recovery or biodiesel production, depending on quality and available outlets.
Do you supply the containers?
Yes: sealed bins and drums, labelled, delivered and maintained by us.
What happens during an inspection?
You present your transfer notes and register. That is precisely what our traceability file is built to cover.

Question not answered here?

Write to us: we reply within 48 working hours.
