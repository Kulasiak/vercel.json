Why now

Algerian regulation has changed direction

Law 25-02 of February 2025, amending and supplementing Law 01-19 on waste management, control and disposal, writes the circular economy into Algerian law: extended producer responsibility (EPR), eco-design, a digital waste management system and tougher penalties.

For a business the meaning is simple: the waste you generate stays attached to you all the way to its final destination. The question is no longer "who takes it away", but "can you prove where it went".

What binds you

  • Law 01-19 as amended by Law 25-02 — waste management and disposal
  • Law 03-10 — environmental protection and sustainable development
  • Executive Decree 13-176 — lubricants and regeneration of waste oils
  • Environmental taxes set each year by the Finance Act
  • SNGID 2035 — target of 47 % recovery of municipal waste
