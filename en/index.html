About us

A neighbourhood bar with a trattoria kitchen

Bar Capri sits in the Esquilino district, on Via Principe Amedeo, where Romans walk to work every morning and travellers arrive straight off the train. We serve both the same way: good ingredients, honest portions, clear prices.

In the kitchen we stay with Roman tradition: crisp guanciale, pecorino grated to order, artichokes in season. At the oven we work a dough that matures for twenty-four hours, because a pizza should be easy to digest.

Guests with particular needs will find gluten-free bread, pasta, pizza and dessert, and a vegan and vegetarian list that is a real choice, not an afterthought.

In short

  • Open every day, from 7:00 to 23:30
  • Kitchen at lunch and dinner, pizza oven lit all evening
  • Menu in 9 languages, by scanning the QR code at the table
  • Around 70 seats, outdoor tables and a room for groups
  • Takeaway and local delivery
