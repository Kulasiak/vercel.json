Eating well near Termini: how to avoid the traps

August 14, 2026 · 2 min read

The station area has plenty of tourist traps and a few excellent tables. Four signs that tell them apart.

There are always places around a station that live on passing trade: you eat there once and never return. But the Esquilino is also a lived-in neighbourhood with a tradition of real trattorias. Here is how to spot them.

The menu is short

A kitchen offering eighty dishes is not cooking all of them to order. A short menu, with a few daily specials, is nearly always a good sign.

Prices are displayed and clear

Cover charge, service and any extras should be written down. If the price of fish is given only per hundred grams without the weight of a portion, ask what it will come to before you order.

Nobody invites you in from the street

Staff stopping passers-by on the pavement is the most reliable sign that a place needs to fill up fast. Good tables have people sitting at them, not standing outside the door.

There are local customers

This is the best test of all: if at lunchtime you see people who work nearby, the value for money holds up. Look at what is on their plates and order that.

We are at Via Principe Amedeo 78/80, five minutes on foot from the station. The menu is the one we always serve, and you can read the prices on this site before you even walk in.
