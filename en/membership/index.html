Membership

Joining means handing your waste oil streams to a single operator and receiving, in return, the documentation that proves your compliance.

Procedure

Four steps

  1. Request

    Online form or a phone call: activity, wilaya, estimated volumes.

  2. Diagnosis

    A site visit within a few working days, then a written proposal.

  3. Contract

    Signature, delivery of containers and a collection schedule.

  4. Follow-up

    A transfer note at every visit and an annual statement of recovered volumes.

What membership includes

A complete service with no hidden costs: containers remain our property and we maintain them.

For large producers, the contract can include payment for the oil collected, depending on volumes and stream quality.

Documents to prepare

  • Trade register extract
  • Tax identification number (NIF)
  • Contact details of the HSE manager or director
  • Estimated monthly volumes
  • Exact address of collection points
