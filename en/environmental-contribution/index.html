Eco-contribution & environmental taxation

In Algeria the polluter-pays principle translates into a set of environmental taxes voted each year in the Finance Act. Here is the practical reading for a business.

What you pay

The main environmental taxes

  • Oils

    Tax on oils, lubricants and lubricating preparations

    Applies to products whose use generates waste oil. The 2026 Finance Act forecasts DZD 7.5 billion in revenue, split 66 % to the State and 34 % to municipalities.

  • Industry

    Tax on polluting or hazardous activities

    Payable by classified installations, according to their nature and capacity.

  • Stockpiles

    Incentive tax on destocking special industrial waste

    Penalises prolonged on-site storage of special waste. 2026 forecast: DZD 550 million, 84 % to the State and 16 % to municipalities.

  • Plastic

    Specific tax on plastic bags

    Applies to bags imported or produced locally.

  • Air & water

    Supplementary taxes: air pollution and wastewater

    Based on industrial discharges exceeding regulatory limits.
