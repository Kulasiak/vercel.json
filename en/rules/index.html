Regulation: Algeria's environment from A to Z

The texts governing waste, waste oils and environmental taxation in Algeria, summarised in plain language. Always check the latest version in the Official Journal (JORADP) before acting.

Reference texts

The legal base

  • 2001

    Law 01-19 of 12 December 2001

    Waste management, control and disposal: duty to sort, recover and dispose of waste in environmentally sound conditions.

  • 2025

    Law 25-02 (Official Journal no. 12, February 2025)

    Amends and supplements Law 01-19: extended producer responsibility, eco-design, a digital management system, phase-out of single-use plastics, revised penalties.

  • 2003

    Law 03-10 of 19 July 2003

    Environmental protection for sustainable development: general framework, prevention and polluter-pays principles.

  • 2013

    Executive Decree 13-176 of 30 April 2013

    Conditions for manufacturing, storing and wholesaling lubricants, and for regenerating waste oils (Official Journal no. 25 of 12 May 2013).

  • 2002

    Executive Decree 02-175 of 20 May 2002

    Creation, organisation and operation of the National Waste Agency (AND), under the environment ministry.

  • Taxation

    Environmental taxes (Finance Acts)

    Tax on polluting or hazardous activities, tax on oils and lubricants, incentive tax on destocking special industrial waste, plastic bag tax, and supplementary taxes on air pollution and wastewater.

  • 2035

    National Integrated Waste Management Strategy (SNGID)

    By 2035: recover 47 % of municipal waste, cut generation below 1.1 kg per inhabitant per day, eliminate illegal dumps and create 30,000 direct jobs.

Who does what

Institutions worth knowing

Ministry of the Environment

Sets national policy and supervises the sector's public bodies.

AND

National Waste Agency: flow data, technical support and recycling promotion.

CNTPP

National Centre for Cleaner Production Technologies: helps industry adopt less polluting processes.

ONEDD

National Observatory for Environment and Sustainable Development: measures and publishes environmental data.

CNFE

National Conservatory for Environmental Training: training and awareness for practitioners.

Wilayas & municipalities

Local permits, municipal waste plans and on-the-ground enforcement.
