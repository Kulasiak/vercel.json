Campaign & slogans

Talking about the environment to the Algerian public takes plain words in everyday language. Here is our message kit, in Standard Arabic and darija, ready for radio, billboards and social media.

Messages

Our slogans

Each slogan is written for a specific medium and a specific audience.

زيتك المستعمل ثروة… لا ترميه

Your waste oil is wealth — don't throw it away.

Lead message · billboards and radio

لتر واحد من الزيت يلوّث ملايين اللترات من الماء

One litre of oil pollutes millions of litres of water.

Educational message · schools and social media

ما ترميش… سلّمهالنا

Don't dump it — hand it to us.

Darija · service stations and garages

من المطبخ إلى الطاقة

From the kitchen to energy.

Cooking oil · restaurants and hotels

الجزائر نظيفة… مسؤولية الجميع

A clean Algeria is everyone's responsibility.

Civic message · municipal campaigns

نجمعو، نرسكلو، نحافظو

We collect, we recycle, we protect.

Darija · radio spots and short video

بيئة نظيفة، صناعة مسؤولة

Clean environment, responsible industry.

B2B message · trade fairs and trade press

كل قطرة تُجمع… مستقبل يُصان

Every drop collected protects a future.

Campaign close · large-format billboards

Channels

Where we carry these messages

Local radio

20-second spots in Arabic and darija on wilaya stations.

Urban billboards

Panels at city entrances, markets, service stations and industrial zones.

Social media

Short vertical demo videos: the right move in ten seconds.

Schools & universities

Workshops and posters, with a practical section on household frying oil.

Collection points

Shared signage on containers, so the right move looks the same everywhere.

Municipal partnerships

Clean-up days with municipalities and neighbourhood associations.

Plan

The campaign in four phases

  1. Understand

    The problem in one number: dumped oil contaminates water and blocks drains.

  2. Show

    The right move, filmed in a kitchen and in a garage — not in a studio.

  3. Make it easy

    Point to the nearest collection point, wilaya by wilaya.

  4. Report back

    Publish the volumes collected: proof that the effort counts.

Run the campaign locally

Municipalities, schools, companies: we provide the artwork and messages, adapted to your area.
