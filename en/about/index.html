About us

EL MAIN LIL SINAA — المعين للصناعة — supports Algerian businesses on one specific point: moving their waste oil out of the informal circuit and back into a clean, documented, legal cycle.

Why we exist

Algeria consumes around 180,000 tonnes of lubricants a year and generates up to 110,000 tonnes of waste oil. A large share is neither collected nor tracked: it ends up burned, poured onto the ground or into sewers. A single litre of oil is enough to contaminate an enormous volume of water.

Our job is to make the right move easier than the convenient one: the right container, a fixed collection date, a document in order.

Principles

Four commitments, on every collection

Compliance first

Permits, transport and destination: everything verifiable, nothing taken on trust.

Real coverage

A network built wilaya by wilaya, with trained local partners.

Written traceability

No collection without a document; no document without an identified destination.

Useful recovery

Re-refining and biodiesel before plain disposal.
